Interviews are a great way to elicit the requirements.  An interview report will require a clear methodology, a list of questions, a list of subjects (don't share identifying information), the raw data (transcript) (as an Appendix), and an analysis of the data.  The analysis is an important component.  The raw data alone is not sufficient.  For the methodology you may wish to consider an empathy map to make sure that you get to all the important aspects.
